Allen’s Camera is a specialist photography retailer founded in Philadelphia in 1977 — one of the most trusted camera stores in the US. For decades, the in-store experience was irreplaceable: knowledgeable staff, expert curation, a community of serious photographers. The challenge was translating that into a product that could compete online without losing what made it special.

**The challenge.** An outdated ecommerce experience was costing real revenue. The existing site was difficult to navigate, the layout confused customers, and online sales were a fraction of what the brand warranted. Allen’s needed more than a redesign — they needed a product that could carry the expertise and trust of the physical store into a digital format.

**What we shipped.** A full ecommerce rebuild on BigCommerce, with a custom CMS giving the Allen’s team genuine editorial control. We built custom search and filtering designed around how photographers actually shop — by system, by use case, by budget — rather than generic category trees. A modern UI that reflected the store’s heritage while feeling competitive with any major online retailer. Then ongoing optimisation sprints to keep pushing the numbers.

**The outcome.** Annual online turnover grew tenfold. 10× growth in online revenue. 22% higher average order value. 197% increase in carts. The results validated what the team already knew — the audience was there, the product just hadn’t been built for them yet.
