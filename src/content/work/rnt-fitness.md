RNT Fitness is a premium fitness education platform built around Akash Vaghela's coaching methodology — multi-tenant, cohort-based, with social learning at its core.

**The challenge.** Building a multi-tenant SaaS platform where coaches, cohorts, and individual clients each had different permission levels, data views, and interaction patterns — without fragmenting the product experience.

**What we shipped.** [FULL_CASE_STUDY_TODO] Full iOS, Android, and web platform from scratch. Multi-tenant architecture with role-based permissions, cohort management, social feed, progress tracking, and coach dashboards.

**The outcome.** Multi-platform product serving thousands of clients across cohorts. Akash's quote: "With all the companies he reviewed, there were always pros and cons for all of them. Except All Shapes."

**Partnership length.** 5 years and ongoing.
