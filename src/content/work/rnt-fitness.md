RNT Fitness is a premium fitness education platform built around Akash Vaghela’s coaching methodology — multi-tenant, cohort-based, with social learning at its core.

**The challenge.** Building a multi-tenant SaaS platform where coaches, cohorts, and individual clients each had different permission levels, data views, and interaction patterns — without fragmenting the product experience. The architecture had to be right from day one, because retrofitting multi-tenancy is one of the most expensive mistakes a product can make.

**What we shipped.** Full iOS, Android, and web platform built from scratch. Multi-tenant architecture with role-based permissions, cohort management, social feed, progress tracking, and coach dashboards. Premium UX designed to match the premium price point of Akash’s coaching programmes.

**The outcome.** A multi-platform product serving thousands of clients across cohorts. The architecture held as the programme scaled. Akash’s advisor reviewed proposals from multiple agencies — he said there were always pros and cons for all of them, except All Shapes.
