The Food Medic is a subscription platform for Dr Hazel Wallace’s evidence-based health brand — 1.2M+ followers across Instagram and TikTok, three best-selling books. We designed and built the native iOS and Android apps members use to follow programmes and stay on track.

**The challenge.** Dr Wallace’s audience didn’t want another generic fitness app; they wanted her method — training, nutrition, and the science behind it — in one place, deep enough to actually follow. The product had to hold structured programmes and educational content together while making day-to-day tracking simple enough that people kept coming back without feeling nagged.

**What we shipped.** A women’s health hub combining training plans, nutrition, tracking, and a 250+ article library. Subscription training and nutrition plans with tiered progress tracking. Native iOS and Android apps built for members who want to go deeper than a feed.

**The outcome.** A daily-use product that turned an audience into a membership — and grew the brand’s recurring subscription revenue in the process.
