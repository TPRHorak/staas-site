The Five Minute Journal is one of the most-downloaded gratitude apps on the App Store — a daily journalling practice distilled into two five-minute sessions. Built for Intelligent Change, the app translates a bestselling physical journal into a product that earns its place in people's morning routines.

**The challenge.** Sustaining a product through every major platform shift while growing from early-adopter tool to mainstream habit. iOS 7 redesign, widgets, Live Activities, Apple Watch, iPadOS, macOS — every new surface needed a considered response that kept the product feeling native without losing the simplicity that made it work.

**What we shipped.** Full redesigns across four major iOS versions. Widget support and Live Activities. Apple Watch integration. Subscription paywall with family sharing. Onboarding personalisation. Multi-platform builds across iOS, Android, iPadOS, watchOS, and macOS. Ongoing maintenance sprints to keep everything sharp.

**The outcome.** 4.8★ average App Store rating. 25,000+ five-star reviews. Regular App of the Day features across multiple markets. 60,000 monthly active users. A product that remains the benchmark for gratitude journalling apps, years after the original launch.
