Mindful Daily Affirmations is an app for Intelligent Change, built from their Mindful Affirmation cards. People choose the themes that matter to them, set gentle daily reminders, and get a steady lift through home-screen widgets — affirmations on display, every day.

**The challenge.** Affirmations only work if they show up consistently and feel personal. An app that buries them behind a launch screen defeats the point. The product had to put the right words in front of the right person at the right moment, without nagging — and let people shape the experience around what they actually care about.

**What we shipped.** Ten affirmation categories — confidence, relationships, health, and more — regularly refreshed so the content doesn’t go stale. Customisable daily reminders. Home-screen widgets that surface affirmations without anyone opening the app. Live on the App Store, free with a subscription tier.

**The outcome.** A calm, daily habit built from a physical product — a steady boost of self-encouragement that lives where people already look, on the home screen.
