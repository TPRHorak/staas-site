The Doctor’s Kitchen is Dr Rupy Aujla’s evidence-based recipe and nutrition platform — turning his bestselling books and podcast audience into a daily cooking habit.

**The challenge.** Building a content-rich recipe app that could support Dr Rupy’s growing audience without becoming a generic recipe aggregator. The product needed to carry the voice and credibility of the brand while delivering personalised, evidence-based nutrition guidance at scale.

**What we shipped.** Full iOS and Android build from scratch. Recipe collections with evidence-based food tagging, personalised meal planning, dietary requirement filtering, subscription paywall, and a content architecture designed for long-form health education. Every interaction designed to feel like guidance from a trusted doctor, not a generic recipe feed.

**The outcome.** Multiple features by Apple — including an editorial feature and App of the Day recognition across markets. 4.8★ rating. 500+ recipes in the library. The product validated Dr Rupy’s approach to food as medicine at consumer scale.
