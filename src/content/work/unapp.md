Unapp was an ambitious productivity platform built around a novel interaction model — a technically complex product that pushed the boundaries of what was achievable on mobile in 2019.

**The challenge.** Building a genuinely novel product interaction with no established patterns to follow. Every architecture decision was a first-principles call.

**What we shipped.** [FULL_CASE_STUDY_TODO] Full iOS build with complex real-time sync, custom interaction model, and multi-service backend. The product launched to strong early traction before Covid disrupted the market.

**The outcome.** A technically accomplished product that demonstrated what's possible when senior engineering judgement is applied to a genuinely novel interaction model.

**Partnership length.** 2 years. Product sunset 2020 due to Covid market conditions.
